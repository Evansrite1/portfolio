Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. They are built so you can get real work done without paying or creating an account.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. The videos, images, PDFs and audio you open are not sent to a server.

Why do some tools take a few minutes?

Your own computer does the work, so a large video takes longer than a small image. Chrome or Edge on a computer works best.
