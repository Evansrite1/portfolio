How to scan a QR code online

  1. Choose a source. Drop or paste an image of the code, pick a file, or press the camera button.
  2. Hold it steady. With the camera, fill the frame with the code and keep it in good light.
  3. Read the result. The content appears on the page. Copy it, or open it if it is a link.

Check links before you open them

A QR code can hide any address. Criminals stick fake codes over real ones on parking meters, posters and menus. Read the address in the result and make sure it is the site you expect before you open it. Never enter passwords or card details on a page you reached from a code you do not trust.

If it will not scan

  • Make sure the whole code is in the picture, including the empty border.
  • Use a sharper, larger image and crop in on the code.
  • Reduce glare and shadows, and hold the camera parallel to the code.
  • Try a different photo. Very small or blurry codes cannot be read.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code scanner free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up and no app to install.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The image is read in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Which codes can it read?

It reads QR codes in every modern browser. In browsers that support the barcode detection feature, such as Chrome on Android and desktop, it also reads many barcode types.

Why does the camera not start?

The browser needs your permission, and the page must be opened over a secure connection. Allow camera access in your browser settings, or upload an image instead.

Is it safe to open a link from a QR code?

Only if you trust where it came from. Read the address first. This tool never opens a link for you.
