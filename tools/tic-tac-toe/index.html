How to play

Take turns putting your mark in a square. The first to get three in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins. If all nine squares fill up with no line, it is a draw.

The three levels

  • Easy: the computer mostly plays at random, so you should win often.
  • Hard: it always takes a winning move and always blocks yours, but it does not plan ahead. Try to make a fork: a move that gives you two ways to win at once.
  • Very hard: it checks every possible game from the current position, so it never loses. Tic-tac-toe is a solved game: with perfect play from both sides it always ends in a draw.

Choose X or O, choose who goes first, or switch to two players and play a friend on the same screen. Your score is kept in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I beat the very hard level?

No. Very hard checks every possible game, so the best result you can get is a draw.

What is the difference between hard and very hard?

Hard always wins when it can and always blocks you, but it only looks one move ahead, so a fork can beat it. Very hard looks at the whole game.

Can two people play?

Yes. Choose A friend under Play against, and take turns on the same screen.

Is my score saved?

Yes, in your own browser, until you press Reset the score.
