Split PDF

Pull pages out of a PDF, split it into several files, delete pages or rotate them. Pick pages by clicking thumbnails. Everything runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded.

Drop a PDF here or choose a file

One PDF at a time. Password protected files must be unlocked first.

Click the pages below to select them, or type page numbers. Hold Shift and click to select a run of pages. Pages keep their original order.

Separate the ranges with commas. Each range becomes its own PDF. 7- means page 7 to the end.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Add your PDF. Drop it on the box or choose it. Every page appears as a thumbnail.
    2. Choose what to do. Extract pages, split by ranges, split every N pages, split into single pages, delete pages or rotate pages.
    3. Pick the pages. Click thumbnails, or type ranges such as 1-3, 5.
    4. Create and download. One result downloads as a PDF. Several results download together as a ZIP.

    Ways to split a PDF

    • Extract pages keeps only the pages you select and saves them as one new PDF.
    • Split by ranges makes one PDF for each range you type. 1-3, 4-6, 7- gives three files.
    • Every N pages cuts the document into equal parts, handy for upload limits.
    • Single pages turns every page into its own PDF.
    • Delete pages removes the pages you select and keeps the rest.
    • Rotate pages turns selected pages by 90 degrees. Rotation also applies to files made in the other modes.

    Tips

    • Large PDFs are fine. Thumbnails are drawn only as you scroll, so even a document with hundreds of pages opens quickly.
    • If the file asks for a password, open it in your PDF viewer, save a copy without the password, and use that copy.
    • Extracted pages keep their original order. To change the order of pages, split them out and then put them together with the Merge PDF tool.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this PDF splitter free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many PDFs you split.

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. The file is split inside your browser on your own device, so it never leaves it. That makes it safe for private documents.

    How do I extract certain pages from a PDF?

    Choose Extract pages, click the thumbnails of the pages you want or type a range such as 1-3, 5, then press Extract pages. You get one new PDF with only those pages.

    How do I split a PDF into several files?

    Use Split by ranges to type the parts you want, Every N pages to cut it into equal parts, or Single pages for one file per page. Several files download together as one ZIP.

    Can I delete pages from a PDF?

    Yes. Choose Delete pages, select the pages to remove and save. The rest of the document is kept in order.

    Can I rotate pages?

    Yes. Choose Rotate pages, select pages and turn them left or right, or rotate every page at once. The rotated PDF is saved as a new file and your original is not changed.

    Why can I not open my PDF?

    The tool tells you if the file is password protected or damaged. For a password protected file, save a copy without the password in your PDF viewer first, then use that copy.
