Crypto price tracker

Live prices for the top 100 cryptocurrencies and any coin you search, with 7 day charts and a watchlist.

Prices from CoinGecko. For information only, not financial advice. Crypto prices can move fast and you can lose money.

Track crypto prices

See the live price, the change over the last hour, day and week, the market cap and a 7 day chart for the top 100 cryptocurrencies. Type any coin name or symbol to find one outside the top 100. Press the star next to a coin to add it to your watchlist, and click a column heading to sort.

Choose the currency you want the prices in, from US dollars to naira, cedi, rand, rupees, dirhams and more. The page refreshes by itself every minute while it is open. Your watchlist and settings stay in your browser.

This tool shows prices and does not give advice. It cannot buy, sell or hold any coins.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the prices come from?

From the free CoinGecko price service, which averages prices across many exchanges.

How often do prices update?

Every minute while the page is open. You can also press Refresh.

Can I see prices in naira?

Yes. Choose NGN, or one of 17 other currencies, in the Show prices in list.

Is this financial advice?

No. It only shows prices. Crypto is risky and you can lose money.
