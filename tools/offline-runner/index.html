About this game

You may know the little runner game that shows up in some browsers when the internet is down. This is an original game in the same spirit, and you can change almost everything: the shape, colour and name of your runner, the world it runs through, how fast it starts and how quickly it speeds up, how high you jump, how strong gravity is, and which obstacles and coins appear.

Jump over pillars, duck under flyers, grab coins for bonus distance and see how far you can go. It runs in your browser, even with no connection once the page has loaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the Chrome dinosaur game?

No. It is an original game in the same spirit, drawn with simple shapes, and it has settings the browser game does not have.

How do I play on a phone?

Tap the game to jump and hold the Hold to duck button to duck.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once the page has loaded. That is what makes it handy when your connection drops.

Is my design saved?

Yes, in your own browser. Press Put everything back to reset.
