Outreach Mailer

Connect up to 25 Gmail accounts, send up to 150 emails a day from each, and read, reply and clean every inbox inside the app. Upload leads from a CSV, text or PDF file, split them across your mailboxes by number, and the app remembers every address it has emailed so none goes out twice. Import your leads, fix their details, write one email and press start. A built-in tracker shows you, live, who opened and who replied.

Download for Windows

Outreach Mailer is for Windows computers for now.

It signs in with a Google app password that you create yourself, and keeps it encrypted on your computer. Your leads and results never leave your computer. Replies, bounces and opt-outs are read from your own mailbox, so those numbers are exact. Opens are a guide, because some mail programs load pictures early or block them.
