Which barcode type should I use?

  • Code 128. Letters, numbers and symbols in a compact code. The best all-round choice for inventory, shipping and internal labels.
  • EAN-13 and UPC-A. Retail product barcodes. EAN-13 is used worldwide and UPC-A in the US and Canada.
  • EAN-8 and UPC-E. Shorter versions for small packages.
  • Code 39. Capital letters and numbers. Common in industry and government.
  • ITF-14. Shipping cartons that hold retail products.
  • Codabar, MSI and Pharmacode. Libraries, shelf labels and pharmaceutical packaging.

How to create a barcode

  1. Pick the barcode type that matches how it will be used.
  2. Enter the value. The tool tells you if it does not fit the type, and adds the check digit for EAN, UPC and ITF-14 when you leave it off.
  3. Adjust the size and colors if you need to.
  4. Download the PNG or SVG and test it with a scanner before printing a full run.

Before you sell in shops

This tool draws any valid number as a barcode. It does not register or license numbers. To sell products through retailers, you normally need product numbers issued by GS1, the organization that manages these numbers. Use this generator for the artwork once you have your numbers, and for internal codes where no registration is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is this barcode generator free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no limits.

What is the difference between EAN-13 and UPC-A?

UPC-A has 12 digits and is used mainly in the US and Canada. EAN-13 has 13 digits and is used worldwide. A UPC-A number can be written as an EAN-13 by adding a leading zero.

What is a check digit?

The last digit of EAN, UPC and ITF-14 numbers is calculated from the others. Scanners use it to catch reading errors. Enter the number without it and this tool adds it for you.

Do I need to register my barcode numbers?

For retail products you usually do, through GS1, so that numbers are unique worldwide. For inventory, tickets and internal labels you can use Code 128 with your own numbers.

Which barcode type is best for inventory?

Code 128 is a good default. It handles letters and numbers and stays compact.

Will my scanner read it?

Barcodes made here follow the standard bar patterns. Print at a good size with strong contrast, keep clear space on both sides, and test with your own scanner.

Should I download PNG or SVG?

Use PNG for documents and screens. Use SVG for printing at any size because it stays sharp.
