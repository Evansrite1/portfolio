Large Lead Finder

Choose what every lead must have: a working email, an Instagram page, or either. Search the niches by typing, pick any countries from the full list of the world (none picked means every country), and set how many leads you want. It reads the open map and each business's own website, collects every social page it finds (Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube and more), checks the emails, and gives you an easy CSV and PDF, plus files ready for the Outreach Mailer and IG Outreach.

Download for Windows

Large Lead Finder is for Windows computers for now. The installer downloads its own built-in browser (about 150 MB), so you need the internet while it installs.

Leads come from OpenStreetMap and from each business's public website. Coverage differs by country: well mapped places give many more leads. Emails are checked for a working mail domain, which lowers bounces but cannot prove a mailbox exists.
