vCard QR code generator

Turn your contact details into a QR code. Anyone who scans it can save your name, phone, email, website and address to their contacts in one tap. Put it on a business card, a poster or an email signature.

How to make a vCard QR code

  1. Fill in your details. Name and at least one way to reach you are enough.
  2. Choose the design. Keep strong contrast so the code scans easily.
  3. Download the code and place it on your card, flyer or signature. You can also save a .vcf contact file.

Tips for a card that scans well

  • Fewer fields make a simpler code. A name, phone, email and website scan far more easily than a full address and long notes.
  • Print the code at least 2 by 2 cm on a business card.
  • Use a short website address.
  • Test it with an iPhone and an Android phone before you print.

Frequently asked questions

What is a vCard?

A vCard is a standard file format for contact details. Phones read it and offer to save the contact.

Do iPhone and Android scan vCard QR codes?

Yes. The built-in camera on recent iPhones and Android phones recognises the code and offers to add the contact.

Is my information safe?

Your details are turned into a code in your browser and are not uploaded or stored by this site. Remember that anyone who scans the printed code can read what is inside it.

Can I update the details later?

No. This makes a static code, so if your number or email changes, make a new code.

What does the .vcf file do?

It is the same contact in a file you can email or import. Tap it on a phone or computer to add the contact.
