How to trim a video

  1. Add your video. Drop it on the box or choose a file. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Choose Keep a selection or Split into two parts. To remove the start and end, keep a selection. To make two files from one, split.
  3. Set your points. Drag the handles on the timeline, or play the video and press Set start here and Set end here.
  4. Trim and download. A lossless cut is almost instant. Save your trimmed MP4 (or the same type of file you added).

Lossless cut or exact cut?

By default the tool copies the video as it is and does not re-encode it. That is fast, even for a very large file, and the picture quality does not change at all. The catch is that a video can only be cut cleanly at a keyframe, a full picture that the frames after it depend on. So a lossless cut snaps to the nearest keyframe before the point you chose, which can be a second or two earlier. The result tells you where the cut really starts.

If you need the cut on the exact frame, turn on Exact cut (re-encode). The tool then re-encodes the video, which takes longer and slightly changes the picture quality, but starts exactly where you said.

Split a video into two parts

Choose Split into two parts, move the split point, and you get two separate videos: everything before the point, and everything after it. This helps with upload limits and with sharing a long recording in pieces. In lossless mode the split point snaps to a keyframe, and the second part starts exactly where the first one ends, so nothing is lost or repeated.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video trimmer free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many videos you trim.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The cut happens inside your browser on your own computer, so the video never leaves your device.

Why is the lossless cut so fast?

It does not re-encode the video. It copies the existing data for the part you keep, so even a large file is cut in seconds and the picture quality stays exactly the same.

Why does my cut start a little earlier than I chose?

A video can only be cut without re-encoding at a keyframe. The lossless cut starts at the nearest keyframe before your start point. If you need the exact frame, turn on Exact cut (re-encode).

What does Exact cut (re-encode) do?

It decodes and re-encodes the video so the cut lands on the exact frame you chose. It takes longer and the video is compressed again, so it is slightly different from the original.

Can I split a video into two parts?

Yes. Choose Split into two parts and move the split point. You get two files, one before the point and one after it.

Which files and browsers work?

You can add MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos. Use Chrome, Edge or another up to date Chromium browser on a computer. The result keeps the same file type when possible.
