Free online video editor

Cut your video, add automatic captions, text and music, pick a filter and save an MP4, on your phone or your computer. Simple to use, and your files never leave your device.

Open the editor

Add your video, photos or music, edit on a simple timeline, and save the finished MP4. It works on a computer and on a phone, and your files never leave your device.

Start editing

Best in Chrome or Edge, on a computer or an Android phone.

What you can do

  • Cut and arrange. Trim the ends of a clip, split it at any point, delete the parts you do not want, and put clips in the order you like.
  • Add text. Titles in several styles, with your own font, colour, size and position.
  • Make captions automatically. The editor listens to the speech in your video and writes the words on the timeline, in English and several other languages. Fix any word, pick a style such as Karaoke, and the captions are saved into your video. You can also type captions by hand, import an .srt file, or download your captions as an .srt file.
  • Add sound. Put in your own music, record a voiceover, and set the volume of every clip.
  • Change the look. Filters, brightness, contrast, colour, warmth, dark edges and zoom.
  • Change the speed. Slow a clip down to a quarter speed or speed it up to four times.
  • Pick the size. YouTube (16:9), TikTok and Reels (9:16), square (1:1) or Instagram post (4:5), with blurred sides or black bars.
  • Add video details. Title, description, who made it, date, copyright, tags and place are saved inside the MP4 file. Private details such as the camera model and GPS place of the original are left out unless you choose to keep them.
  • Save an MP4. Pick 1080p or 720p and a quality level. The editor shows the expected file size before you start.

How to make a video

  1. Add your files. Choose videos, photos or music from your device, or drop them on the page.
  2. Edit on the timeline. Tap a clip to select it. Use Split, Delete, Copy, Earlier and Later, and drag the white ends of a clip to trim it.
  3. Use the tools at the bottom. Text, Captions, Sound, Filters, Adjust, Speed, Size and Details each open a short panel.
  4. Add captions. Open Captions, choose the language and press Make captions automatically. Check the words, then pick a look.
  5. Export. Tap Export, pick the quality, wait for it to finish and download your MP4.

The first time you open the editor it offers a 30 second guided tour. You can start it again any time from the question mark button at the top.

What to know before you start

  • Everything is made on your own device, so a long or high resolution video takes a few minutes to export. Keep the tab open while it works.
  • Very large projects need a computer with plenty of memory. Full HD with a handful of clips is comfortable.
  • Automatic captions download a 40 MB speech model the first time, then work from your browser. They are most accurate with clear speech, so always read them through. Make captions after you finish cutting, because they do not move when you cut the video.
  • Your project is not saved if you close the tab, so export before you leave.
  • Videos in formats your browser cannot play, such as some iPhone HEVC files on older computers, cannot be opened. Use the video converter first.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video editor free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many videos you make.

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Your files are opened and edited on your own device and the finished video is made there too, so nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The editor is built for phones as well as computers. Use Chrome or Edge, or another current Chromium browser, on an Android phone or a computer. Other browsers may not be able to export.

Does it make captions automatically?

Yes. Open Captions and press Make captions automatically. A speech model that runs in your browser listens to the speech in your video and voiceover and writes the words with their timing. It works best with clear speech in English and is less accurate in other languages, so check and fix the words. You can also type captions yourself or import an .srt file.

Are my captions or audio sent anywhere?

No. The speech model is hosted on this site and runs on your own device. Your video and sound are never uploaded.

Can I download the captions as a subtitle file?

Yes. The Captions panel and the export screen both offer an .srt file you can upload to YouTube and other sites.

Can I put my own music in the video?

Yes. Add an audio file from your device or record a voiceover. The editor does not include a music library, so use tracks you have the rights to.

What does the video details panel do?

It saves a title, description, author, date, copyright, tags and place inside the MP4 file, plus a cover picture. Copyright, tags and place are stored together in the comment field.

Why does exporting take a while?

Your own device does the work, so a long or high resolution video takes a few minutes. A faster computer with a graphics card is quicker.

What file types can I use?

MP4, MOV, WebM and other video your browser can play, plus JPG, PNG and WebP photos and MP3, WAV and M4A audio. The finished video is saved as MP4.
