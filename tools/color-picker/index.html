How to pick a color

  1. Choose a hue. Move the rainbow slider, then click in the large square to set how bright and how strong the color is.
  2. Or start from something you have. Type a hex code, an rgb() or hsl() value or a name such as tomato. Load a photo and click any pixel, or use the screen eyedropper in browsers that support it.
  3. Copy the value you need. HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV and CMYK are shown together, each with a copy button.
  4. Save it. Keep the colors you like and copy them all as CSS variables.

Get a color palette from an image

Load a logo, a product photo or a screenshot and the tool finds its main colors using median cut, a standard way to reduce an image to a few representative colors. The most common color comes first. Click a swatch to use it, or save the whole palette.

Check that your text is readable

The contrast checker follows the WCAG guidelines. Normal text needs a ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 for level AA and 7 to 1 for level AAA. Large text, which is 24 pixels or 19 pixels bold and above, needs 3 to 1 for AA and 4.5 to 1 for AAA. Buttons, icons and form borders need 3 to 1. Black on white scores 21 to 1, the highest possible.

Build a color scheme

  • Complementary. The color opposite on the wheel. Strong contrast, good for a call to action button.
  • Analogous. Neighbors on the wheel. Calm and harmonious.
  • Triadic. Three colors evenly spaced. Lively but balanced.
  • Split complementary. A color plus the two neighbors of its opposite. Contrast with less tension.
  • Shades and tints. Darker and lighter versions of one color, useful for hover states and backgrounds.

Frequently asked questions

Is this color picker free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit. It runs in your browser.

How do I get the HEX code of a color?

Pick the color or type it in, and the HEX code is shown in the values panel with a Copy button.

How do I find a color from an image?

Load the image in the Pick from an image section and click the pixel you want. The tool also builds a palette of the main colors in the picture.

What is the screen eyedropper?

In Chrome and Edge on a computer, a button lets you pick a color from anywhere on your screen, even outside the browser. If your browser does not support it, the button is hidden and the image picker still works.

What contrast ratio do I need?

For normal text, 4.5 to 1 passes WCAG level AA and 7 to 1 passes AAA. Large text needs 3 to 1 for AA. Black on white is 21 to 1.

What is the difference between HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK?

HEX and RGB describe a color as amounts of red, green and blue and are used on screens and in CSS. HSL describes hue, saturation and lightness, which is easier to adjust by hand. CMYK is for print. The CMYK shown here is a simple calculation and a printer may give a slightly different result.

Are my saved colors private?

Yes. They are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing your browser data removes them.
