    How to add a watermark to a photo

    1. Add your photos. Drop one or many images on the box, or paste them.
    2. Choose text or a logo. Type your name or brand, or upload a logo. A PNG logo with a transparent background looks best.
    3. Style it. Pick the font, color, size, opacity and rotation. The preview updates as you move the controls.
    4. Place it. Choose one of nine positions, or tile the watermark across the whole image.
    5. Apply and download. The same watermark is added to every image. Save them one by one, or as a single ZIP.

    Tips for a watermark that works

    • Tile it to protect a photo. A single mark in a corner is easy to crop out. A repeated, slightly see-through, rotated mark across the image is much harder to remove.
    • Use a corner mark for branding. A small logo in the bottom right corner with 60 to 80% opacity looks professional and does not spoil the picture.
    • Keep it readable. Turn on the outline for text so it shows on both light and dark areas.
    • Size is relative. The size is a share of each image width, so a batch of photos in different sizes all get a watermark of the same look.
    • Save a copy. Keep your original photos. A watermarked file is a new file and the original is never changed.

    Common uses

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this watermark maker free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no limit on images and no watermark of ours added to your pictures.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The watermark is drawn in your browser on your own device. Your images are never sent to a server.

    Can I watermark many photos at once?

    Yes. Add all the images, set the watermark once, press Apply to all, then download each file or a ZIP of everything.

    Can I use my logo instead of text?

    Yes. Choose Logo or image as the type and upload your file. A PNG with a transparent background works best, and you can set its size, opacity and rotation.

    How do I make a watermark harder to remove?

    Turn on the repeat option, rotate the mark a little, and use a medium opacity. A mark that covers the whole picture is much harder to crop or edit out than one in a corner.

    Will it change the size of my photo?

    No. The output has the same width and height as the original. Choose PNG for the sharpest result or JPG for a smaller file.

    Does it change the original file?

    No. Your original stays as it is. You download new files with -watermarked added to the name.
