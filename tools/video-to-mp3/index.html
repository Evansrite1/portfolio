How to convert a video to MP3

  1. Add your file. Drop a video on the box or choose a file. Audio files like WAV and M4A work too.
  2. Pick MP3 or WAV and a bitrate. 192 kbps is a good balance. Use 320 kbps for the best quality, or 128 kbps for the smallest file.
  3. Trim if you want only part of it (optional). Type a start and end time, or play the file and press Set start here and Set end here.
  4. Convert and download. The tool shows the length and size of your MP3, plays it back, and lets you save it.

Which bitrate should I choose?

  • 128 kbps: small files, fine for speech, podcasts and lectures.
  • 192 kbps: a good all round choice for music and everyday use.
  • 256 kbps: high quality music that is still easy to share.
  • 320 kbps: the highest MP3 quality, with the largest file.

A higher bitrate cannot add detail that is missing from the original sound. If the video has low quality audio, 320 kbps only makes a bigger file.

MP3 or WAV?

MP3 is small and plays on every phone, computer and car stereo. WAV is uncompressed, so the file is much larger, but it is a good choice when you plan to edit the audio further in another program.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video to MP3 converter free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many files you convert.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The audio is extracted inside your browser using your own computer, so the file never leaves your device.

Which video and audio files can I convert?

MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos, and audio files such as WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and MP3. If your browser cannot read the audio inside a file, the tool tells you.

What bitrate should I pick for MP3?

Use 192 kbps for a good balance of quality and size, 128 kbps for speech and the smallest file, and 320 kbps for the best quality.

Can I save only part of the audio?

Yes. Type a start and end time, or play the file and press Set start here and Set end here. Leave them empty to convert the whole file.

Can I save the audio as WAV instead?

Yes. Choose WAV for an uncompressed file that is easy to edit in other programs. WAV files are much larger than MP3.

Which browsers work?

Use Chrome, Edge or another up to date Chromium browser on a computer. Older browsers and some phones cannot do the conversion and will show a message.
