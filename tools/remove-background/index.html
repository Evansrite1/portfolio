How to remove a background

  1. Add your image. Drop it on the box, paste it, or choose a file.
  2. Wait a moment. The tool picks the best method for your picture and shows the result on a checkerboard so you can see what is transparent.
  3. Fine tune. Use the sliders if some background is left or the edge needs to be softer or tighter.
  4. Download. Save a transparent PNG, a JPG on a solid color, or copy the image.

Which method does it use?

  • Plain background (exact). For logos, icons, signatures and graphics on a white or single-color background. The background color is removed from the edges inward, so the result is clean and instant, and nothing needs to download.
  • Photo (AI cutout). For people, products and animals. An open-source AI model finds the main subject. The first time you use it, the engine (about 15 MB) downloads once and is then kept by your browser.
  • Automatic. Looks at the edges of your image and chooses between the two for you. Images that are already transparent are left alone.

Tips for a clean cutout

  • Use a sharp image where the subject stands out from the background.
  • For photos with several objects, crop in on the one you want. The AI model keeps the most prominent objects it finds.
  • For fine hair or fur, soften the edge a little and check the result on a dark and a light background.
  • Logos work best as a high-resolution image on a plain background.

Frequently asked questions

Is this background remover free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed in your browser, so it never leaves your device.

Why is the first photo slower?

The AI engine, about 15 MB, downloads the first time you cut out a photo. After that your browser keeps it, so later photos are fast. Images on a plain background need no download at all.

Why did it keep other objects in my photo?

The model finds the most prominent objects in a picture, not just one. Crop the image around the subject you want and try again.

Which file formats work?

You can add PNG, JPG and WebP images. Downloads are a transparent PNG or a JPG on a solid color.

Does it work for logos?

Yes, and it works best for them. Logos on a plain background are cleaned exactly, without the AI model.

Which model does the AI cutout use?

The open-source U2-Net model, in its small version, running through ONNX Runtime Web in your browser.
