        Go live online

        A web page cannot send video to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or TikTok on its own. The free program OBS Studio does that, and it can show exactly what this presenter shows.

        1. Make sure the TV screen has opened (it opens by itself when the app starts, or after your first click).
        2. In OBS, press the plus under Sources, choose Window Capture and pick the window named Display screen.
        3. In OBS, open Settings, Stream, pick your platform and paste the stream key from its live page.
        4. Press Start Streaming in OBS.
        YouTube LiveFacebook LiveTwitchTikTok LIVEGet OBS

        To keep a copy instead, use the red record button. It saves a video of what is on the screen to this computer. Direct streaming from the page itself would need a paid relay service, which can be added later.
