How to compress a video

  1. Add your video. Drop it on the box or choose a file.
  2. Pick a size. Choose Smallest, Balanced or Best quality, or set the exact size you need.
  3. Trim or split (optional). Drag the handles to cut off the start and end, or turn on Cut into two parts and move the split marker.
  4. Compress and download. It takes a few minutes. Then save your video, or both parts.

How to get a 2 GB video down to 500 MB

Pick Balanced, which aims for about a quarter of the original size, or drag the size slider to the exact number you need. For a smaller file with a sharper picture, also lower the resolution to 720p. A phone or laptop screen rarely shows the difference.

Cut a video into two parts

Some sites and chat apps limit upload size. Turn on Cut into two parts, set the split point, and the tool creates two separate videos, each compressed to share the size you chose.

Tips for the best result

  • Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and older browsers are not supported.
  • Keep the tab open and let your computer stay awake while it works.
  • Choose MP4 unless you know you need WebM. MP4 plays on almost every device.
  • If the result looks soft, raise the size a little or lower the resolution.

Frequently asked questions

Is this video compressor free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many videos you compress.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The video is compressed inside your browser using your own computer, so it never leaves your device.

How much smaller can I make a video?

You choose the size. A 2 GB video can usually be reduced to about 500 MB and still look the same on a phone or laptop screen. The tool shows an estimate of the quality before you start.

Can I cut a video into two parts?

Yes. Turn on Cut into two parts, move the split marker, and you get two separate files, each compressed. This helps with upload limits.

Can I trim the start or end of a video?

Yes. Drag the handles on the timeline, or play the video and use Set start here and Set end here.

Why does it take a few minutes?

Your own computer does the work, so a long HD video takes a while. Chrome and Edge on a computer with a graphics card are fastest.

Which browsers and formats work?

Use Chrome, Edge or another up to date Chromium browser on a computer. You can add MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos, and download MP4 or WebM.
