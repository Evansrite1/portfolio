Choir Assistant

Play a keyboard with five sounds, find the key of a song, and drop in any song to see where the bass, chords, lead line and beat play, with plain-language notes on what you are hearing. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Keyboard

Octave 4

Click or tap the keys, or use your computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K L play the white keys and W E T Y U O P play the black keys. Z and X change the octave, Space is sustain.

Song key finder

Pick where the music comes from. The tool listens and works out the key from the notes it hears. Keep it going for 10 to 20 seconds for a better answer.

Song breakdown

Drop in a song and see what plays when: the sections, the chords, the lead line (usually the voice), the bass and the beat. Press play and follow along. The keyboard above lights up with the chord, the bass note and the lead note you are hearing.

Or drop a song hereMP3, WAV, M4A, OGG. It is read on your device and never uploaded. A 4 minute song takes about 10 to 30 seconds.
