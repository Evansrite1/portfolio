Act one

Where the business is today

Seven things I found, in the order a new client runs into them.

i.

Your front door asks a stranger for €350

The one link in your Instagram bio opens the 1:1 Clarity Session: 75 minutes, €350. For someone who found you five minutes ago, that is a big first step. Meanwhile you already have a free 15-minute discovery call on the same booking profile, and another one on your website. Neither is linked from Instagram, so the easiest yes you offer is hidden.

Bio link: cal.eu/marcelavarianiconsultancy/claritysession → "1:1 Clarity Session (60–90 min)", 1h 15m, €350.00
Also on the same profile: "Free Discovery Call", 15m, not linked from Instagram
ii.

Four doors, and all of them lead into your DMs

Your recent posts ask people to DM COACH, THEATER, THAILAND or DUBAI. Each one is a real offer, but none of them has a page. Someone who is curious about the Thailand retreat at 11pm has to message you and wait, with nothing to read in the meantime: no dates, no price range, no photos of the venue, no stories from past participants. A lot of interest quietly cools off in that gap.

iii.

Most of your 21.5K followers aren't seeing you

Your recent posts reach a small slice of your audience. Across five recent posts, likes ranged from 3 to 50. For an account your size, a commonly cited healthy range is 1 to 3% of followers engaging per post. You're sitting well under that.

Two likely reasons. Many of your followers came from earlier chapters of your life (a lot of the comments are in Portuguese and Spanish), while your offers now speak to women in Dubai. iv.

Your credentials are almost invisible

Very few coaches can say what you can: a degree in Philosophy and Human Sciences (PUCRS, Brazil), a specialization in Education for Expression (Roma Tre, Italy), a Master's in Philosophy for Children (Universidade dos Açores), Therapeutic Theater training at Espailudic, the Gestalt School of Catalunya, and Mindfulness training in Italy. Today they appear only halfway down your website's homepage. They're not in your Instagram bio, not on your booking page, and not in your pinned posts.

v.

The brand is split five ways

A client can meet you as @inlovewithmarcela on Instagram, ILWM at inlovewithmarcela.com, Marcela Variani Consultancy on your booking page and corporate site, @thesoftceomanifesto (32 followers, no posts yet), and a corporate wellness coach on LinkedIn. Your booking page also lists tarot readings (€45 to €115) right next to relationship coaching. Each piece makes sense to you. To a stranger, it's hard to tell which Marcela to book.

Two booking systems in use: Cal.com (Instagram) and Calendly (website)
vi.

The website is between chapters

You mentioned you're rebuilding, so this is only a list for the new version. Today the "1:1 Relationship Coaching" page shows "Something new is coming". The homepage headline is "Welcome here!". There are no testimonials even though your "Wins" highlight is full of them. There's no email signup, no link back to Instagram, and no page for the Thailand retreat, the Dubai workshops or Therapeutic Theater.

vii.

Dubai can't find you on Google yet

I searched "relationship coach Dubai women workshop". The first ten results were other coaches and directories (Hub of Consciousness, Coach G, Lighthouse Coaching, Dubai Life Coach, Noomii and others). You weren't on the page. For in-person work in a new city, that search is where many clients start.

Act two

What it should be doing

A checklist you can work through yourself, grouped by what you asked about: more visibility, more influence, more money.

More visibility Be found

  • One "Start here" link in your bio with four clear doors: book a free call, Therapeutic Theater sessions, the Thailand retreat, Dubai workshops. The DM keywords can stay; they just point to a page instead of a waiting inbox.
  • Rename your pages for the words clients search. "Welcome here!" becomes something like "Relationship Coach for Women in Dubai". Give every page its own title and description with Dubai, Barcelona and online.
  • Create a free Google Business Profile in Dubai. It's the fastest route onto the map results for "relationship coach near me".
  • Make Therapeutic Theater the headline. It's your best-performing content and the most distinctive thing you offer. One page that explains what happens in a session, with photos, will travel further than any generic coaching page.
  • Borrow audiences in Dubai. You already collaborate with Roam Circle on the retreat. Do the same with women's communities, yoga studios and co-working spaces: one hosted Therapeutic Theater evening puts you in front of a room that already trusts the host.

More influence Be trusted

  • Put your credentials where decisions happen: one line in your Instagram bio ("Philosophy MA · Gestalt Therapeutic Theater"), and a short block on every booking page.
  • Turn your "Wins" highlight into written testimonials with first names and photos, and place three on each offer page. Your corporate site already has strong ones you can reuse.
  • Choose one name for the public. Keep Marcela Variani Consultancy for companies, but let clients meet one brand: In Love with Marcela. Move tarot to its own page so it doesn't compete with coaching.
  • Refresh your following over time. Speak to the women you serve now (Dubai, English, Therapeutic Theater) and let the audience rebalance around them. Remove obvious spam comments so real ones stand out.

More money Be booked

Give people a ladder to climb instead of one steep step. Something like this, using offers you already have:

Free
A short self-check or guide ("What pattern keeps repeating in your relationships?") in exchange for an email. This builds your list.
Free · 15 min
Discovery call, linked from your bio.
€350
Clarity Session. Offer to credit it toward a longer program if they continue, which makes the €350 an easy decision.
Program
Your 1:1 coaching container, sold on its own page with outcomes, length and testimonials.
Retreat
Thailand, December, 6 to 10 women. With 8 seats, every €500 on the price is €4,000 in revenue, so a proper page that justifies a premium price matters.
Companies
Corporate wellness and team workshops through Marcela Variani Consultancy.
  • Start a retreat waitlist today: a simple form (name, email, "what are you hoping to leave behind?"). A waitlist fills seats before the page even goes public, and the answers write your marketing for you.
  • Make Dubai workshops recurring and ticketed, for example a monthly Therapeutic Theater circle with a fixed number of seats, booked and paid online.
  • Reply to every DM keyword automatically with the matching page, then follow up personally. Nobody waits, and you still get the conversation.
  • Use one booking system. Cal.com or Calendly, not both, so reminders, payments and your calendar all live in one place.
Act three

What changes once it's fixed

More first conversations. A woman who connects with a reel can book a free call in two taps, instead of facing a €350 decision on day one.

Fewer lost leads. Every DM keyword returns a page with the answer, so interest doesn't cool off while you sleep.

Easier sales. Credentials and testimonials do the persuading before the call starts, so calls become about fit, not about proving yourself.

A retreat that fills from a list, with a price that reflects what it is, rather than from DMs one by one.

A presence in Dubai that shows up on Google and in other people's rooms, not just on Instagram.

Week 2

Therapeutic Theater page and retreat page. First hosted workshop with a partner.
