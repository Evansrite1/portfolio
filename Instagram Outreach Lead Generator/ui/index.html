Checking

Setup

1What do you offer?

Pick your service. The app keeps only the businesses that need it.

2Who do you want to reach?

Choose the kinds of business, where they are, and how big their account is.

3Your message

Every lead gets a message. Leave this empty and it is written for you from what the app found. Or write your own.

You can use {first_name} {niche} {city} {country} {handle} {website} {instagram_link}. Only say what is true: the app never claims you built or prepared anything.

4Run it

This uses your own Instagram login in a real browser window. Safe checks one profile every few seconds and stops at a daily limit that protects your account. Fast is quicker but raises the chance Instagram limits the account.
