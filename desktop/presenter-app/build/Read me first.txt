How to install on a Mac (Apple chip or Intel)
==========================================

This takes about two minutes. You only do it once.

STEP 1. Install the app
  - Open the file that ends in .dmg (it is in this folder).
  - Drag "Presenter by Evans Large" onto the Applications folder.

STEP 2. Tell your Mac it is safe (needed once)
  Your Mac shows a warning for apps that do not come from the Apple store.
  The app is fine. Do this one time:
  - Press Command and Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  - Paste this line and press Enter:

      xattr -cr "/Applications/Presenter by Evans Large.app"

STEP 3. Open it
  - Open the Applications folder and double-click "Presenter by Evans Large".
  - If the Mac still says it cannot verify the developer:
      1. Press Done.
      2. Open the Apple menu, then System Settings, then Privacy and Security.
      3. Scroll down and press "Open Anyway" next to Presenter.
      4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then open the app again.

AFTER THAT
  - It opens like any other app, and it works with no internet.
  - When the internet is on, it checks for updates by itself and shows an
    "Update now" button. Press it and the app updates and opens again.
    You never need to come back to the website.

WHICH DOWNLOAD DID I NEED?
  Apple menu, then About This Mac.
    "Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4"  ->  use the Apple chip download
    "Processor: Intel"              ->  use the Intel download
