Websites that bring in customers

I build with real code, so the site loads fast and can grow past its first version instead of hitting the limits of a template.

How a project runs

  1. Scope and price. We talk through what you need. Then I send a clear proposal with exactly what gets built and exactly what it costs, agreed before any work starts.
  2. Build. Working software, not mockups. You see it take shape and tell me what to adjust along the way.
  3. Handover and changes. You get the finished system with a walkthrough. Any change you need after launch gets made.
  4. Review. Once it is running, you tell others how it went.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a web developer based in Lagos?

Yes. I am based in Lagos, Nigeria, and I build websites and web apps for clients in Nigeria and around the world.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is scoped and quoted individually. The price depends on how many pages and features it needs, the integrations such as payments or booking, and the timeline. You get a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical build takes two to three weeks from the first scope call to launch.

Do you use templates or write real code?

Real code is where I usually land, because it gives a site room to grow. I use no-code tools when they get you there faster and fit the need.

Can you update or fix my existing website?

Yes. I take over existing sites and apps, find what is wrong and fix it without breaking the rest.
