Instagram plan
Nov 2026 to Apr 2027

100 posts: Monday value carousel, Wednesday silent African market Reel, Thursday "Built For" showcase, Saturday myth or story carousel. Open a month, copy a caption, download the slides, schedule on Instagram, tick it off.

How to use each month
  1. Pick the month. Open the first post.
  2. Press Download slides (a zip of 1080 by 1350 images), then Copy caption and hashtags.
  3. On Instagram: New post, choose the slides in order, paste the caption, Advanced settings, schedule for 8:00 PM your time.
  4. Tick Scheduled. Ticks are saved in this browser only.
Instagram limits how far ahead you can schedule (about 30 days has worked before), so do one month at a time. Hashtags: 30 on every post, the most Instagram allows.
