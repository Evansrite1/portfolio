Setup

1What must every lead have?

Leads that miss it are left out, so you never get an empty row.

2Which niches?

Start typing and pick from the list, or type your own word and press Enter. Pick as many as you like. 0 chosen

3Where?

Pick one or more countries. If you pick none, every country in the world is searched.

4How many leads?

Some places have fewer matching businesses on the map. The app tells you when it runs out.

5What comes with each lead?

Business, niche, country, city, email, phone, website and every social page found (Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Telegram, Threads) always come. Tick the extras you want in the spreadsheet.

6Skip leads I already have

Choose an earlier spreadsheet. Its emails and Instagram handles are not found again.
