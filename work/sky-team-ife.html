The problem

An organization running multiple centers had no single view of how each location was actually performing. Attendance was tracked inconsistently, weekly reports were manual, and there was no way to compare centers against each other or spot which office needed attention without someone compiling numbers by hand.

What I built

  • A per-user sign-in system with accounts scoped by role and by center, enforced at the database level with Supabase row-level security, not just hidden in the UI.
  • Attendance capture and weekly reporting that replaces manual compilation with a live, structured record.
  • Office rankings and center analytics so leadership can see performance across locations at a glance.
  • A static, no-build front end deployed on Vercel, no server to maintain, cheap to run, fast to load.

Why it matters

Leadership now has one place to check how every center is doing, backed by data that is entered once and enforced by real access controls instead of trust. What used to take manual follow-up across locations is now a dashboard anyone with access can open.
