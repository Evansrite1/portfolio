Chill Wireless

Built for Jake Hassan: a storefront for a wireless carrier selling unlimited premium plans on the nationwide 5G network, designed to convert a visitor straight into a signed-up subscriber.

The problem

A wireless carrier needed a storefront that could do the job a phone store normally does: explain the plan clearly, build trust fast, and get a visitor to check out, without a salesperson walking them through it.

What I built

  • A clear, price-led homepage that leads with the offer, unlimited premium data on the nationwide 5G network, before anything else competes for attention.
  • Trust signals (top rated, fully secured, real support) placed right next to the offer instead of buried in a footer.
  • A plans page and checkout flow built for someone deciding on their phone in under a minute.
  • Live chat wired in so a visitor with a question never has to leave the page to get an answer.

Why it matters

The site carries the entire sales conversation that used to require a store visit or a phone call, from first impression to checkout.
