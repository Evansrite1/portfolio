01Client portal

CareTRAC

Care General Construction

A construction company running several job sites had no shared system between the crew and the client. Now every project has one place for progress, photos, approvals and messages.

The problem
Site updates, photos and approvals lived in separate text threads and phone calls, so homeowners felt out of the loop and details slipped between the crew and the office.
What I built
A secure portal with two experiences: contractors log progress and photos, homeowners track milestones and review documents, with messaging inside and access based on role.
The result
Every project now has one shared record that both sides can see.

02Membership platform

Billionaire Club

A business education community

A community that teaches people to build income-earning skills, and then trains its strongest members to lead the next group. I turned that whole journey into a real product.

The problem
The journey from understanding the model, to building skills, to leading others was scattered across chat groups, PDFs and one-off calls.
What I built
A Learn, Build, Lead flow inside the product, a landing experience that takes a visitor from what is this to start my journey, accounts that put returning members in the right stage, and content the client can edit without a developer.
The result
One place where a prospect lands and immediately understands what they are signing up for.

Editorial brand photographer, Leeds
