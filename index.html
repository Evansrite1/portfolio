About

Full stack developer

I’m a full stack developer and the CEO of Large Technologies. I can build whatever you can imagine.

I spend my time looking at businesses: how they take bookings, answer leads, send invoices and keep their customers, and where money quietly slips through. I work across real estate, hospitality, healthcare, events, education and more. The businesses I work best with share one thing: the bottleneck is never effort, it is systems. I build the system.

    Handover and changes
