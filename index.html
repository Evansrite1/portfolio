About

CEO, Large Technologies

I am Evans Ademilua, a software builder and the CEO of Large Technologies, the development intelligence, in Lagos. I spend my time doing one thing well: taking a business that is stuck running on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and manual follow-ups, and replacing that mess with a piece of software that actually fits how the business works.

I build with real code, no-code tools and AI-assisted "vibe coding," whichever gets you there fastest. Code is usually where I land though, it gives a system room to grow past its first version instead of hitting a wall the moment you need something a template does not support.

I work across multiple industries, real estate, hospitality, healthcare, events, education and beyond. The businesses I work best with all share one thing: the bottleneck is never a lack of effort, it's a lack of systems. I build the system.
