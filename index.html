About

CEO, Large Technologies

I am Evans Ademilua, a software builder and the CEO of Large Technologies, the development intelligence, in Lagos. I spend my time doing one thing well: taking a business that is stuck running on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and manual follow-ups, and replacing that mess with a piece of custom software that actually fits how the business works.

I am not a no-code template shop. Every build is written in real code, so it can grow past its first version instead of hitting a wall the moment you need something the template does not support. That is the difference between a tool that looks good in a demo and one your team still relies on a year later.

Most of the founders and teams I work with are in real estate, hospitality and other service businesses where the bottleneck is never a lack of effort. It is a lack of systems. I build the system.
