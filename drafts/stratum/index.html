Part 1: What we found

Your reputation is ahead of your website.

Stratum is one of the longest established owners association managers in Dubai. A board member who finds you today meets a site that does not yet show it. Every point below was checked on 13 October 2026.

01

On a phone, the site is the desktop page made small

The site has no mobile setting, so a phone shows the full desktop layout shrunk to fit. Text is tiny and the menu is cramped. Most board members and owners will read it on a phone.

Checked in two browsers. See the screenshot below.
02

There is no easy way to call you

No phone number on the homepage, no tap-to-call, no WhatsApp. The number appears only as plain text on the contact page. The contact form offers "Request a phone call" but has no phone number field.

03

The news section stops in 2015

The latest article is from May 2015 and still calls Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed the Crown Prince. A visitor could easily assume the company is no longer active, even though you are expanding and hiring.

04

Boards looking for a manager do not find you

A search for "owners association management Dubai" returns Strata Global, Nakheel Strata, PID OAM, Mansions and Prime Association Management. Stratum, with more associations served than most of them, is not on the page.

05

Nothing on the homepage proves your results

No buildings named, no results, no board testimonials, and every banner says "Learn more". There is no clear action for a board that wants a proposal. The homepage "Association Managers" link also leads to a page that does not exist, and the brochure on offer is from 2012.

Part 3: How it makes Stratum more money

Seven changes, each aimed at winning or keeping a building.

New buildings

"Get a proposal for your building"

A short form asking for the building, number of units and contract renewal date. It alerts your team on WhatsApp and email at once and confirms to the board within a minute. Every board that is unhappy with its manager becomes a lead you can follow up the same day.

New buildings

Be found for the searches boards make

Pages built to rank for "owners association management Dubai", "change OA management company Dubai", "Mollak service charge" and similar, written from your existing glossary and FAQs, which are already good material.

Trust

A proof page

Buildings and communities you manage, results such as service charge budgets reviewed and collection rates, board testimonials, the Dubai Police award and your independence promise, all in one place a board can forward to its members.

Retention and pitches

A modern owner portal

A branded front door for owners and boards showing service charge against budget, the maintenance log and the general assembly pack. It also becomes a live demo you can open in any pitch. Your current login can stay behind it.

Reach

An Arabic version

Many owners and board members prefer to read in Arabic. A full Arabic homepage and key pages widen the number of buildings that can trust you at first glance.

Momentum

News and newsletter that run

A short monthly update on JOP law, Mollak and building care, published on the site and emailed to the list you already collect. It shows an active company and keeps you in front of every board.

Hiring

A careers page that recruits

You are expanding. A clear page for Association Managers and Building Supervisors with a simple application, so good people do not have to guess whether you are hiring.

Every change

Measured every month

A one-page monthly report: how many proposal requests came in, from which pages and searches, and how many became meetings, so we keep doing what brings buildings.
