Straight answers

Questions boards ask before they switch manager.

How do we change our owners association manager?

Tell us your building and when the current contract renews. We review the management agreement and budget, explain the notice requirements and propose a clear handover plan so there is no gap in service.

What does an owners association manager actually do?

We run the finances (budgets, service charges and the trust account), convene the general assembly and board meetings, keep the records RERA expects, and oversee maintenance and suppliers on behalf of the owners.

Why does independence matter?

Because the manager who also sells you facilities management or legal work has a reason to recommend it. Stratum has no such partnerships, so our advice is only ever for the owners.
