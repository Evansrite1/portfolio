From a strong practice to the region's reference firm

OCG-PRO already has what most firms lack: a clear position (only on the investor's side), real crisis experience and a steady stream of expert content. This page covers what is holding the website back today, and the moves that turn that position into more mandates. The concept site is here.

Part 1: Checked on ocg-pro.com, 12 October 2026

What visitors see today

01

The site stays on the loading logo for about two minutes

The loading screen waits until every image has finished downloading. One photo, about.jpg, is 1.6 MB and the server sends it slowly, so an investor clicking from your LinkedIn post sees only the OCG logo, and many visitors will leave before the page appears.

img/about.jpg: 1,608,163 bytes. Test 1: 112.8 s. Test 2: 118.7 s. Test 3: stopped at 150 s. For comparison, a construction site that won an Awwwards nomination this month loaded its homepage in 1.4 s on the same connection.

Fix: compress the image to under 150 KB, serve images from a fast CDN, and show the page immediately instead of waiting for every image.

02

A banner says the site is in test mode

A moving banner on the homepage reads "САЙТ РАБОТАЕТ В ТЕСТОВОМ РЕЖИМЕ!" (the site is working in test mode). For a firm that sells control and reliability to capital owners, it is the wrong first impression.

Fix: remove it, or finish the pages it is waiting for.

03

Your strongest numbers start at zero

15+ years, 42 projects and 20 engineers are in the page, but the counters start at 0 and only count up after the loading screen clears. Anyone who leaves early, or any search engine reading the page, sees zeros.

Fix: print the real numbers and animate them on top, not instead.

04

Your posts promise tools the site does not have

Recent posts offer a Delay Cost Calculation model and a tender audit, both by email. There is nowhere on the site to get either, so every interested reader has to write a cold email first.

Fix: put both on the site as working tools that capture the enquiry, as in the concept.

Part 2: The next level

Eight moves that win more mandates

01

Make oversight visible

A live investor view on every mandate: verified progress against drawdown, the acceptance trail, open items with owners and dates. Investors stop trusting a summary and start seeing evidence weekly.

Retainers renew on what clients can see working. It also becomes the reason to choose OCG-PRO over a firm that sends PDFs.

02

Build the lender channel

EBRD, IFC, ADB and commercial banks need independent Owner's Representatives before they release capital. A dedicated lender page, a bankability checklist for developers, and a lender login to the same investor view.

One bank relationship can refer many projects. It is the highest leverage source of mandates in the region.

03

Package an entry offer

Clear, fixed-scope products: Tender Audit, Pre-Development Review, Full Owner's Representative, Crisis Turnaround. Each with what is delivered and how long it takes.

A small first purchase, like a tender audit, is easy to say yes to and leads naturally into the full mandate.

04

Turn tools into a pipeline

The delay calculator and the tender check each ask for an email, send the result instantly, and start a short follow-up sequence. Every enquiry lands in one list with its stage.

Readers who are not ready today are still in your pipeline next quarter, instead of lost.

05

Publish once, rank in three languages

You already write a strong post every few days. Each becomes an article on the site in English, Russian and Uzbek, aimed at searches like технический надзор Ташкент and owner's representative Uzbekistan.

Content that is found on Google keeps bringing enquiries long after a LinkedIn post has scrolled away.

06

A case study library

The $25M turnaround is your best sales asset. Five more like it, each with the numbers: claims rejected, CAPEX saved, months recovered, plus a quote from the investor where possible.

Capital owners buy proof. Specific numbers shorten the decision.

07

Answer in minutes

Every enquiry alerts you on Telegram the moment it arrives, and the sender gets an immediate reply confirming a senior engineer will respond within one working day.

The first credible reply usually wins the meeting.

08

Measure what brings work

Simple tracking of which posts, pages and tools produce enquiries, reviewed monthly.

You spend writing time on the topics that actually bring investors to you.
