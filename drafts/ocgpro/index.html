The problem we exist to solve

Your only window is written by the party you are paying.

When the owner and the contractor sign progress acts between themselves, the investor receives a report, not evidence. Overruns stay invisible until the money runs out.

Budget control does not start in accounting. By the time an inflated invoice reaches the accountant, the capital is already committed. It starts on site, with volumes measured against the bill of quantities before an act is signed.

Case study | Commercial asset, Uzbekistan

A $25M project without independent governance

+40%Budget overrun, over $10M of unplanned capital
18 moDelay to commercial commissioning
3Root causes, all preventable
  1. Bypassed pre-development. High groundwater found during excavation forced an emergency foundation redesign.
  2. Conflict of interest in acceptance. Acts signed without independent verification of volumes or estimates.
  3. No Owner's Representative. The investor saw unverified reports until financial exhaustion.

OCG-PRO's crisis unit restructured the documentation, introduced tranche-based disbursement and an independent governance framework. The project was stabilised and completed.
