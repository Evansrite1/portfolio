The Inquiry System

Turn more of the couples who find you into booked weddings, inside the Showit site you already have. No redesign.

What happens today

  • Your inquiry form asks only for name, email, subject and message. No wedding date, venue, guest count, budget or phone.
  • The message box placeholder shows a code fragment: "Write your message here.&nbsp;"
  • Nothing replies until you do. Couples who message three photographers usually book whoever answers first with real information.
  • There is no tracking on the site, so you can't see which posts, pages or venues bring inquiries.

What I'll build

  1. A smarter inquiry form. Wedding date, venue or location, guest count, budget range, phone, and "how did you find me". Placeholder fixed. Matches your current design.
  2. An instant, on-brand reply. The moment a couple inquires, they receive a warm email from you with your collections guide and a link to book a short call, so they never wait.
  3. Automatic follow-up. If they go quiet, a gentle check-in on day 2 and day 5, written in your voice. It stops as soon as they reply or book.
  4. Lead tracking. Every inquiry lands in one simple pipeline (inquiry, call booked, proposal sent, booked) so nothing slips.
  5. Analytics + Meta Pixel. Know which posts and pages bring inquiries, and remind engaged couples who visit but don't inquire.
