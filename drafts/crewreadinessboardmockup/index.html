The six squares are the stations a crew member has been signed off on: front counter, drive-thru, grill, fries, prep, close. Solid means signed off, faded means part done, empty means not started. Ready now means every station signed off plus a shift lead sign-off outstanding. The column that usually surprises owners is the last stat, the people who have not moved in sixty days. Nobody decided to stall them. They just stopped being the person a manager happened to mention.
