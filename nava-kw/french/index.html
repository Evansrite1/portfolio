French

The classic tip, redrawn.

What it is

One line, many versions

A French manicure is a soft nude base with a clean line of colour along the free edge. Sanaz keeps the shape classic and changes everything else: her French highlight shows orange tips and soft pink fades, and her feed shows red French and chrome tips.

The line is drawn by hand, so it follows the curve of each nail, almond or stiletto.

Her versions
  • 01Red French, a deep red tip on a sheer nude base.
  • 02Orange French, a bright tip with a soft, modern edge.
  • 03Soft pink fade, a gentle gradient from nude to pink.
  • 04Chrome tip, a mirror finish on the free edge, with lime accents.
