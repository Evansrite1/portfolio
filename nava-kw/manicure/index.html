Russian manicure

Manicure

Clean, precise, done the Russian way.

What it is

A dry technique with a fine finish

Russian manicure is a dry technique. Instead of soaking and cutting, the cuticle area is cleaned with a fine electric file and the nail is shaped by hand. The result is a smooth, tidy line around every nail and a clean base for colour.

Sanaz shows it up close in her Manicure highlight on Instagram, with the tag “Russian manicure” and the signature Sanaz.nails.art.

How it goes
  • 01The cuticle area is cleaned with a fine electric file.
  • 02The nail is shaped to the length you want, from short almond to long stiletto.
  • 03Colour or a design is added, from nude glaze to cat eye and chrome.
  • 04The finish is sealed, glossy or matte.
From her page

Every tool is disinfected and sterilised before it touches a client, in the autoclave she shows in her Sterilize highlight.
