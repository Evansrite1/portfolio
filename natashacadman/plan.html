02: The real constraint

The ceiling isn't demand. It's days.

03

£ = D × V

Revenue is shoot days times value per day. As a mum of three, Natasha can't sell many more days. So every lever in this plan does one of three things: raises value per day, earns without a new day (recurring and digital), or fills the days she has with better-paying clients.

£450

Typical value per shoot today (working assumption)

A mix of Portrait and Content Edits, plus editing and admin time that never gets billed.

~2 hrs

Lost per booking to email back-and-forth

Every "enquire for details" means quotes, dates and a deposit chased by hand. That's unbilled time.

0

Income that arrives without a shoot

No retainer, no membership, no team contract. Every month starts at zero.

Strategy 1: Raise value per day

Build a price ladder people climb.

04

Today the Edits jump from £300 to £895 with nothing above. Add a top tier so £895 looks like the sensible middle, and make the Content Edit the obvious choice. The jump in average order value comes from structure, not from working more.

Strategy 2: Earn without a new shoot day

The Brand Library: a membership.

05

Her best clients need fresh images every season, not once. Turn that into a quarterly membership: four short seasonal sessions a year, batched into two studio days per quarter, so members are shot back to back.

£195

Per month

12-month term. Four seasonal mini shoots, 20 images each, priority dates.

Why it works: SkinWorx, Studio 490 and her other repeat brands already come back. A membership turns "we should book Tash again" into a standing order, and it smooths the quiet months.

Strategy 5: Visibility that ends in a booking

A demand engine, not a feed.

08

01

One clear position

"Editorial brand photography for female founders in Leeds." Remove the wedding-era titles and URLs, redirect them, and split family work onto its own page so Google knows which Natasha to send people to.

02

Case studies that rank

A page per client (SkinWorx, Studio 490, Ariel White, Laura Penrose) targeting "[industry] brand photographer Leeds". Each one is proof and a search entry point, and clients share their own.

03

Freebies to the Content Edit

One form for the Posing Guide, 25 prompts and the checklist, then four emails over ten days ending in a Content Edit offer. The bio link does one job: grow the list.

04

A referral circle

Web designers, VAs, business coaches and stylists in Yorkshire refer founders for 10% or a reciprocal shoot. Five partners sending one client a quarter is twenty bookings a year.

Seasonal

Black Friday gift vouchers, sold to the list

Search
