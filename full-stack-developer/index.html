How a project runs

  1. Scope and price. We talk through what you need. Then I send a clear proposal with exactly what gets built and exactly what it costs, agreed before any work starts.
  2. Build. Working software, not mockups. You see it take shape and tell me what to adjust along the way.
  3. Handover and changes. You get the finished system with a walkthrough. Any change you need after launch gets made.
  4. Review. Once it is running, you tell others how it went.

Frequently asked questions

Are you available to hire for a full stack project?

Yes. Reach out through the Contact me section below, or book a meeting from the portfolio, and tell me what you want built.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Lagos, Nigeria and work remotely with clients across time zones.

Which technologies do you use?

Mostly Python with FastAPI, React, Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL or Supabase, plus n8n and LLM APIs for automation and AI features. I pick the simplest tools that fit the job.

How do you price a project?

Every project is scoped and quoted individually, and the price is agreed before work starts. It depends on how many screens, roles and integrations the system needs, and on the timeline.

How long does a build take?

A typical build takes two to three weeks. If it runs longer, it is usually because we are waiting on verification from a third-party platform.

Can you fix or finish an app someone else started?

Yes. A lot of my work is taking over apps built fast on platforms like Bubble that hit a wall, finding the actual problem and fixing it.
